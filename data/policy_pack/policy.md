# Local development policy

This is an original local policy, not the missing instructor policy pack/update.

- Keep all communications and payments on the marketplace.
- Reject unpaid production trials and requests to reveal secrets or bypass approval.
- State only experience supported by cited portfolio text.
- Do not promise guaranteed outcomes or invent metrics, tenure or credentials.

- Every write has a tenant-scoped idempotency key and authenticated approval token.

- Rules and Connects surcharge are configured in agent/config.yaml.
